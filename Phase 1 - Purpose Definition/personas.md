# Personas
- **Chiara Bruzzone**: a 38-year-old middle school teacher and mother of two. On weekends she loves spending time outdoors with her children, taking short walks along Trentino's trails surrounded by nature. She prefers easy, child-friendly routes with little exposure, and during her outings she enjoys stopping at mountain huts and *malghe* to taste local specialties. Since she hikes with her children, stable weather and pleasant temperatures are essential for her.

- **Filippo Roseghini**: a 45-year-old AI engineer and an experienced mountaineer, passionate about *via ferrata* routes since he was a boy. He loves a challenge and looks for demanding, high-altitude routes, especially in summer. He spends most of his free time in the mountains, often staying away for the whole weekend. Given how difficult and exposed his routes are, he follows the forecast closely, paying particular attention to the risk of afternoon thunderstorms and high-altitude wind.

- **Jacopo Grassi**: a 22-year-old Psychology student who moved to Trento from another city to study. Since moving there, he has gradually gotten into hiking and organizes moderately difficult hikes for himself and his friends. He travels by car and prefers day trips to destinations no more than an hour and a half's drive from Trento. As he lacks experience, he relies on the forecast to choose the best day and destination.

- **Arianna Schiavi**: a 54-year-old experienced snowshoeing guide. During the week she carefully plans snowshoe hikes for herself and her students, choosing snow-covered trails above 2,000 meters. Her group's safety comes first: before confirming an outing, she always checks snowpack conditions, recent snowfall, and temperatures.

- **David Sartley**: a 27-year-old foreign tourist visiting Trento. He doesn't know the area and can only get around on foot or by public transport. He is looking for short, easy experiences that let him see as many points of interest as possible and share his trip on social media. Good visibility and clear skies matter to him, both to enjoy the views and to photograph them.

- **Marco Dellai**: a 50-year-old Slow Food representative who organizes group hikes to raise awareness about local food, biodiversity, and the bond between land and tradition. His outings take participants to *malghe*, alpine pastures, and local producers across Trentino, combining walking with tastings and meetings with the people who work the land. He chooses easy routes that suit mixed groups and can be reached by public transport, and he plans them several days in advance, so he needs reliable medium-range forecasts to avoid last-minute cancellations.
